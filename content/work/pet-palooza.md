## Deep Analysis of the game, Adopt Me! {#n-448988f7d205}

<p id="n-df13f8e0f0b0">First, let&#x27;s start with an overview of Adopt Me. Adopt Me is a popular Massively Multiplayer Online Role-Playing Game (MMORPG) developed by the Roblox Corporation. Calling Adopt Me a popular game would be a profound understatement. At the time of writing this document, <span class="nc nc-yellow"><strong>Adopt Me has amassed a whopping total of a 32.5B visits with a peak of 1.6m+ concurrent players. </strong></span>That&#x27;s more than 4x the world population! Now let’s break that down, why a game designed for players aged 7+ gained such popularity.</p>

<p id="n-590216ba2151"><span class="nc nc-purple"><strong>Reasons for the Popularity (Mechanics-Wise):</strong></span></p>

<ol><li id="n-f0bfeb0a0a30">The game was initially launched with a goal where <span class="nc nc-yellow"><strong>players would be able to adopt kids</strong></span>, but later evolved into a game where <span class="nc nc-yellow"><strong>players can also roleplay as adoptive parents to different types of virtual pets</strong></span>, such as dogs, cats, unicorns, and dragons.</li><li id="n-232a1a8a26c1">Kids love <span class="nc nc-yellow"><strong>adopting a pet and taking care of them.</strong></span></li><li id="n-8a16d9075218">The game also allows them to <span class="nc nc-yellow"><strong>design their own homes, and explore various locations</strong></span> in the game&#x27;s world. </li><li id="n-9a8cb128846b">It also has pets in which you can level up by grinding out exp which gives you money to get rarer ones.</li><li id="n-3a106ce2d084">The players are able to <span class="nc nc-yellow"><strong>buy and sell pets, and trade items</strong></span>. </li><li id="n-e07eadf2eaa5">There are also <span class="nc nc-yellow"><strong>small little mini-games</strong></span> to play around the map.</li></ol>

<p id="n-ae6d4127f7b6"><span class="nc nc-purple"><strong>Reasons for the Popularity (Experience-Wise):</strong></span></p>

<ol><li id="n-9f7d18673c4c">A very polished game with an <span class="nc nc-yellow"><strong>attracting art style </strong></span>that appeals to children.</li><li id="n-bc5d35df9c7e">The <span class="nc nc-yellow"><strong>soundtrack and the sound effects</strong></span> are very fitting.</li><li id="n-3dfc1bbe49cc"><span class="nc nc-yellow"><strong>“Family” feel with tasks</strong></span> that can help players take care of their children and pets.</li><li id="n-38d298f8621f">Family function in which you can <span class="nc nc-yellow"><strong>adopt other players</strong></span>.</li><li id="n-b1357eac3435">It&#x27;s simply <span class="nc nc-yellow"><strong>made to chill</strong></span>.</li></ol>

<p id="n-b525b17c7760"><span class="nc nc-purple"><strong>Reasons for the Popularity (Community-Wise):</strong></span></p>

<ol><li id="n-a7f80192e12e"><span class="nc nc-yellow"><strong>Frequent updates</strong></span> with a fresh stream of content.</li><li id="n-65bf919ef62b">Combination of an <span class="nc nc-yellow"><strong>avid community</strong></span> of players and <span class="nc nc-yellow"><strong>freeform play</strong></span>, similar to a semi-open world games.</li><li id="n-cffe88b9290b">The game’s <span class="nc nc-yellow"><strong>text-chat function</strong></span> is monitored and <span class="nc nc-yellow"><strong>limited</strong></span> in language, so there’s little risk that children will be <span class="nc nc-yellow"><strong>exposed to harmful content</strong></span>.</li></ol>

<p id="n-a847becb0f18">The <span class="nc nc-yellow"><strong>simplicity </strong></span>is what attracts kids to play the game for hours on hours. It&#x27;s fun to <span class="nc nc-yellow"><strong>level up the pet to make Mega Pets</strong></span> and have an awesome collection. <span class="nc nc-yellow"><strong>This evokes a sense of &quot;satisfaction&quot; in many players, compelling them to return for more</strong></span><strong>. </strong>All in all, the team found a good formula and stuck with it. <span class="nc nc-yellow"><strong>These reasons combined</strong></span> was the <span class="nc nc-yellow"><strong>high-poin</strong></span><span class="nc nc-yellow"><strong>t</strong></span> for the <span class="nc nc-yellow"><strong>game’s popularity</strong></span>. This game rapidly became a <span class="nc nc-yellow"><strong>prime social hub</strong></span> for kids to hangout with friends, or to unwind after a long studious day. The cherry on the top is, <span class="nc nc-yellow"><strong>you can do most of these things without making additional purchases</strong></span> and most players don’t spend anything on the game – <u><em>but as you progress, there is a drive to sell items that enable you to access rarer pets.</em></u></p>

<p id="n-ca98aa4d4c5c">With any good product comes a fair share of shortcomings. <span class="nc nc-yellow"><strong>According to my findings and playtime</strong></span>, there is <span class="nc nc-yellow"><strong>not much learning</strong></span> and the game has become more of a <span class="nc nc-yellow"><strong>trading platform</strong></span> to say the least. There are a couple of <span class="nc nc-yellow"><strong>scams </strong></span>that happen <span class="nc nc-yellow"><strong>more often</strong></span> than we’d think. These scams include <span class="nc nc-yellow"><strong>cloning a pet, trading for Robux, and levelling up a pet</strong></span>. </p>

<p id="n-f4fc88cd0891"><strong>Lets discuss some of the shortcomings of the game. </strong></p>

<p id="n-7d3ed99596ed"><span class="nc nc-purple"><strong>Shortcoming #1: </strong></span>The game is a <span class="nc nc-yellow"><strong>grind fest</strong></span>, where the player needs to put in a<span class="nc nc-yellow"><strong> lot of time and efforts</strong></span> to get a <span class="nc nc-yellow"><strong>legendary</strong></span> pet, which is considered a <span class="nc nc-yellow"><strong>very valuable item</strong></span> in the game. <span class="nc nc-yellow"><strong>Mega Neon Diamond Griffin</strong></span> requires you to put in a minimum of <span class="nc nc-yellow"><strong>1170 consecutive days of playing</strong></span>.</p>

<p id="n-c1dbdbe1fa95"><span class="nc nc-purple"><strong>Why: </strong></span>There are <span class="nc nc-yellow"><strong>five ranks of pets</strong></span>: Common, Uncommon, Rare, Ultra-Rare and Legendary. The most valuable legendary pets include <span class="nc nc-yellow"><strong>Dragons, Unicorns, Griffins, Ninja Monkeys and Kitsunes</strong></span>. Getting these takes <span class="nc nc-yellow"><strong>effort and a lot of time</strong></span>. Once acquired, you can train your pets by completing tasks. You spend your <span class="nc nc-yellow"><strong>entire time micromanaging</strong></span> your pet (and/or baby) so you can <span class="nc nc-yellow"><strong>earn currency to buy things</strong></span>. Once you have <span class="nc nc-yellow"><strong>four fully grown pets</strong></span>, they can be traded for even <span class="nc nc-yellow"><strong>rarer Neon pets</strong></span> with glowing parts. You can <span class="nc nc-yellow"><strong>repeat this process four times</strong></span> to trade them in for a <span class="nc nc-yellow"><strong>Mega Neon Pet</strong></span> that glows in rainbow colors. As your pet grows, they can <span class="nc nc-yellow"><strong>learn tricks</strong></span>, which are pretty neat until you realize that they add almost nothing other than a <span class="nc nc-yellow"><strong>cosmetic value</strong></span> to the game. </p>

<p id="n-d4d913ac1dcc"><span class="nc nc-purple"><strong>Shortcoming #2: </strong></span>A kids game primarily <span class="nc nc-yellow"><strong>doesn’t</strong></span> need a <span class="nc nc-yellow"><strong>trading</strong></span> system because of <span class="nc nc-yellow"><strong>complexity and unfairness</strong></span>.</p>

<p id="n-8a4642e4818e"><span class="nc nc-purple"><strong>Why: </strong></span>Players can <span class="nc nc-yellow"><strong>trade pets</strong></span> with each other. <span class="nc nc-yellow"><strong>Trading </strong></span>may be <span class="nc nc-yellow"><strong>difficult </strong></span>for younger kids to understand, leading to <span class="nc nc-yellow"><strong>frustration, confusion, and potential conflicts</strong></span> among players. Additionally, trading can create an <span class="nc nc-yellow"><strong>unfair environment</strong></span> in games, especially if some players have <span class="nc nc-yellow"><strong>access to better items or pets</strong></span> than others, leading to <span class="nc nc-yellow"><strong>feelings of exclusion or resentment</strong></span>. There are also safety concerns, as trading may encourage players to <span class="nc nc-yellow"><strong>share personal information</strong></span> or engage in risky behaviors to obtain rare items, putting them at risk of <span class="nc nc-yellow"><strong>cyberbullying or online predators</strong></span>. </p>

<p id="n-92973efc85b8"><span class="nc nc-purple"><strong>Shortcoming #3: </strong></span><span class="nc nc-yellow"><strong>In-App purchases</strong></span> to have an edge, bringing you closer to <span class="nc nc-yellow"><strong>“winning”</strong></span> the game. This creates a sense of <span class="nc nc-yellow"><strong>“Gambling”</strong></span> touch to the game. This whole thing can be termed as a <span class="nc nc-yellow"><strong>“Pay-2-Win”</strong></span> situation.</p>

<p id="n-e644bb1a69bf"><span class="nc nc-purple"><strong>Why: </strong></span>Players receive money from <span class="nc nc-yellow"><strong>daily award, periodic checks, and completing pet tasks</strong></span>. However, unless they <span class="nc nc-yellow"><strong>play every single day</strong></span>, its a <span class="nc nc-yellow"><strong>slow and long grind</strong></span> to get more expensive things. This ecosystem of pets <span class="nc nc-yellow"><strong>encourages extended play</strong></span> time as well as the <span class="nc nc-yellow"><strong>purchasing of currency</strong></span>. While the game may be <span class="nc nc-yellow"><strong>virtual</strong></span>, the money is <span class="nc nc-yellow"><strong>real</strong></span>. Eggs, <span class="nc nc-yellow"><strong>Gift Boxes</strong></span>, etc. that have a posted 2-8% chance of getting a rare or a legendary items. This brings in a <span class="nc nc-yellow"><strong>“Gambling”</strong></span> touch to the game. The game also present the player with the <span class="nc nc-yellow"><strong>premium items first</strong></span> discouraging them from <span class="nc nc-yellow"><strong>designing their in-game avatar</strong></span>. If they&#x27;re not paying attention, they will <span class="nc nc-yellow"><strong>think </strong></span>that those are the <span class="nc nc-yellow"><strong>only items in the entire catalog</strong></span>. Furthermore, other concerns are <span class="nc nc-yellow"><strong>scams </strong></span>and <span class="nc nc-yellow"><strong>price-hiking</strong></span>, with items deliberately priced to make players buy <span class="nc nc-yellow"><strong>more currency</strong></span> than they <span class="nc nc-yellow"><strong>require</strong></span>. This is a classic example of <strong><span class="nc nc-yellow">Ladder Strategy</span></strong>. <em>Ex: A pet might cost 1000 Robux, but you can only buy 800 ($9.99) or 1700 ($19.99) Robux at a time. </em></p>

## A Take on Financial Literacy in a Kids Game {#n-c9a29f326e1e}

<p id="n-bc84889dac80">Although we would <span class="nc nc-yellow"><strong>not want to use Trading in the game</strong></span>, we will use <span class="nc nc-yellow"><strong>Monetization through in-app purchases</strong></span> to teach kids about <span class="nc nc-yellow"><strong>financial responsibility</strong></span> in a very easy way, at an young stage. Incorporating elements of <span class="nc nc-yellow"><strong>money management and savings</strong></span> into the in-app purchases of the game could be a <span class="nc nc-yellow"><strong>valuable educational feature</strong></span>. Here are some potential ideas for how to implement this:</p>

<p id="n-3eb934b3c3be"><span class="nc nc-purple"><strong>Concept of virtual currency:</strong></span><span class="nc nc-purple"> </span>At the start of the game, players could be introduced to the <span class="nc nc-yellow"><strong>concept of virtual currency</strong></span> and how it can be used to buy different items in the game. The game could explain that players <span class="nc nc-yellow"><strong>earn </strong></span>virtual currency by <span class="nc nc-yellow"><strong>completing tasks</strong></span> and <span class="nc nc-yellow"><strong>achievements</strong></span> in the game or by <span class="nc nc-yellow"><strong>purchasing it with real money</strong></span>.</p>

<p id="n-55f35f60786f"><span class="nc nc-purple"><strong>Set savings goals:</strong></span><span class="nc nc-purple"> </span>The game could encourage players to <span class="nc nc-yellow"><strong>set savings goals</strong></span> by displaying the cost of virtual items and <span class="nc nc-yellow"><strong>encouraging players to save up</strong></span> to buy them. <em>For example, the game could offer a special breed of an animal/pet that costs 1,000 virtual coins, and players could be encouraged to save up to buy it at instead of spending their coins on other items. </em>The game could <span class="nc nc-yellow"><strong>reward players</strong></span> for saving their virtual currency by <span class="nc nc-yellow"><strong>offering bonuses or discounts on certain items</strong></span> when purchased with <span class="nc nc-yellow"><strong>saved coins</strong></span>. <em>For example, the game could offer a 10% discount on a pet bed if purchased with saved coins instead of purchased with real money.</em></p>

<p id="n-f9ce9e2c6de7"><span class="nc nc-purple"><strong>Introduce a virtual savings account: </strong></span>The game could introduce a <span class="nc nc-yellow"><strong>virtual savings account</strong></span> where players can <span class="nc nc-yellow"><strong>deposit </strong></span>their virtual coins and <span class="nc nc-yellow"><strong>earn interest</strong></span> over time. The game could also introduce the concept of <span class="nc nc-yellow"><strong>earning interest on savings</strong></span>, and explain how saving money can lead to <span class="nc nc-yellow"><strong>future benefits</strong></span>. The game<strong> </strong>could encourage responsible spending by <span class="nc nc-yellow"><strong>displaying</strong></span> the <span class="nc nc-yellow"><strong>total amount of virtual currency</strong></span> players have <span class="nc nc-yellow"><strong>earned and spent</strong></span>. The game could also display a <span class="nc nc-yellow"><strong>breakdown </strong></span>of how much players have <span class="nc nc-yellow"><strong>spent on in-app purchases</strong></span>, and encourage them to <span class="nc nc-yellow"><strong>think carefully</strong></span> before making future purchases.</p>

<p id="n-e882ca61051a">By incorporating these elements into the in-app purchases of the game, <span class="nc nc-yellow"><strong>young kids</strong></span> can <span class="nc nc-yellow"><strong>learn </strong></span>about <span class="nc nc-yellow"><strong>money management</strong></span> and <span class="nc nc-yellow"><strong>savings </strong></span>in a fun and engaging way while <span class="nc nc-yellow"><strong>still enjoying the game</strong></span>.</p>
