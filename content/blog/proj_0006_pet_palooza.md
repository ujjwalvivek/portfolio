## Deep Analysis of the game, Adopt Me! {#n-448988f7d205}

<p id="n-df13f8e0f0b0">First, let&#x27;s start with an overview of Adopt Me. Adopt Me is a popular Massively Multiplayer Online Role-Playing Game (MMORPG) developed by the Roblox Corporation. Calling Adopt Me a popular game would be a profound understatement. At the time of writing this document, Adopt Me has amassed a whopping total of a 32.5B visits with a peak of 1.6m+ concurrent players. That&#x27;s more than 4x the world population! Now let’s break that down, why a game designed for players aged 7+ gained such popularity.</p>

<p id="n-590216ba2151"><strong>Reasons for the Popularity (Mechanics-Wise):</strong></p>

<ol><li id="n-f0bfeb0a0a30">The game was initially launched with a goal where players would be able to adopt kids, but later evolved into a game where players can also roleplay as adoptive parents to different types of virtual pets, such as dogs, cats, unicorns, and dragons.</li><li id="n-232a1a8a26c1">Kids love adopting a pet and taking care of them.</li><li id="n-8a16d9075218">The game also allows them to design their own homes, and explore various locations in the game&#x27;s world. </li><li id="n-9a8cb128846b">It also has pets in which you can level up by grinding out exp which gives you money to get rarer ones.</li><li id="n-3a106ce2d084">The players are able to buy and sell pets, and trade items. </li><li id="n-e07eadf2eaa5">There are also small little mini-games to play around the map.</li></ol>

<p id="n-ae6d4127f7b6"><strong>Reasons for the Popularity (Experience-Wise):</strong></p>

<ol><li id="n-9f7d18673c4c">A very polished game with an attracting art style that appeals to children.</li><li id="n-bc5d35df9c7e">The soundtrack and the sound effects are very fitting.</li><li id="n-3dfc1bbe49cc">“Family” feel with tasks that can help players take care of their children and pets.</li><li id="n-38d298f8621f">Family function in which you can adopt other players.</li><li id="n-b1357eac3435">It&#x27;s simply made to chill.</li></ol>

<p id="n-b525b17c7760"><strong>Reasons for the Popularity (Community-Wise):</strong></p>

<ol><li id="n-a7f80192e12e">Frequent updates with a fresh stream of content.</li><li id="n-65bf919ef62b">Combination of an avid community of players and freeform play, similar to a semi-open world games.</li><li id="n-cffe88b9290b">The game’s text-chat function is monitored and limited in language, so there’s little risk that children will be exposed to harmful content.</li></ol>

<p id="n-a847becb0f18">The simplicity is what attracts kids to play the game for hours on hours. It&#x27;s fun to level up the pet to make Mega Pets and have an awesome collection. This evokes a sense of &quot;satisfaction&quot; in many players, compelling them to return for more. All in all, the team found a good formula and stuck with it. These reasons combined was the high-point for the game’s popularity. This game rapidly became a prime social hub for kids to hangout with friends, or to unwind after a long studious day. The cherry on the top is, you can do most of these things without making additional purchases and most players don’t spend anything on the game – <em>but as you progress, there is a drive to sell items that enable you to access rarer pets.</em></p>

<p id="n-ca98aa4d4c5c">With any good product comes a fair share of shortcomings. According to my findings and playtime, there is not much learning and the game has become more of a trading platform to say the least. There are a couple of scams that happen more often than we’d think. These scams include cloning a pet, trading for Robux, and levelling up a pet. </p>

<p id="n-f4fc88cd0891">Lets discuss some of the shortcomings of the game. </p>

<p id="n-7d3ed99596ed"><strong>Shortcoming #1: </strong>The game is a grind fest, where the player needs to put in a lot of time and efforts to get a legendary pet, which is considered a very valuable item in the game. Mega Neon Diamond Griffin requires you to put in a minimum of 1170 consecutive days of playing.</p>

<p id="n-c1dbdbe1fa95"><strong>Why: </strong>There are five ranks of pets: Common, Uncommon, Rare, Ultra-Rare and Legendary. The most valuable legendary pets include Dragons, Unicorns, Griffins, Ninja Monkeys and Kitsunes. Getting these takes effort and a lot of time. Once acquired, you can train your pets by completing tasks. You spend your entire time micromanaging your pet (and/or baby) so you can earn currency to buy things. Once you have four fully grown pets, they can be traded for even rarer Neon pets with glowing parts. You can repeat this process four times to trade them in for a Mega Neon Pet that glows in rainbow colors. As your pet grows, they can learn tricks, which are pretty neat until you realize that they add almost nothing other than a cosmetic value to the game. </p>

<p id="n-d4d913ac1dcc"><strong>Shortcoming #2: </strong>A kids game primarily doesn’t need a trading system because of complexity and unfairness.</p>

<p id="n-8a4642e4818e"><strong>Why: </strong>Players can trade pets with each other. Trading may be difficult for younger kids to understand, leading to frustration, confusion, and potential conflicts among players. Additionally, trading can create an unfair environment in games, especially if some players have access to better items or pets than others, leading to feelings of exclusion or resentment. There are also safety concerns, as trading may encourage players to share personal information or engage in risky behaviors to obtain rare items, putting them at risk of cyberbullying or online predators. </p>

<p id="n-92973efc85b8"><strong>Shortcoming #3: </strong>In-App purchases to have an edge, bringing you closer to “winning” the game. This creates a sense of “Gambling” touch to the game. This whole thing can be termed as a “Pay-2-Win” situation.</p>

<p id="n-e644bb1a69bf"><strong>Why: </strong>Players receive money from daily award, periodic checks, and completing pet tasks. However, unless they play every single day, its a slow and long grind to get more expensive things. This ecosystem of pets encourages extended play time as well as the purchasing of currency. While the game may be virtual, the money is real. Eggs, Gift Boxes, etc. that have a posted 2-8% chance of getting a rare or a legendary items. This brings in a “Gambling” touch to the game. The game also present the player with the premium items first discouraging them from designing their in-game avatar. If they&#x27;re not paying attention, they will think that those are the only items in the entire catalog. Furthermore, other concerns are scams and price-hiking, with items deliberately priced to make players buy more currency than they require. This is a classic example of Ladder Strategy. <em>Ex: A pet might cost 1000 Robux, but you can only buy 800 ($9.99) or 1700 ($19.99) Robux at a time. </em></p>

## Ideation for the New Game and Analysis of Roblox as a Game Engine {#n-c67ed03a9946}

<p id="n-178a0a414f91"><strong>Reasons to use Roblox as a Game Platform for younger kids (6yrs-8yrs):</strong></p>

<ol><li id="n-30c53f7a7c06">According to a report from <a href="https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=_gXlauRB1EQ&amp;t=9s" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">People Make Games</a>, Roblox sees an estimated 200 million users a month and its core user base is between the ages of 6 and 15.</li><li id="n-89568d4b7c9e">Roblox is like a blank canvas to start with, giving a creative freedom.</li><li id="n-a754166e61d1">Easier to create multiplayer games without dealing with complicated network code or server hosting.</li></ol>

<p id="n-de25c80fcc29">We will leverage these advantages of Roblox as a platform to create a new game for kids between the ages 6 and 8, giving them more learning opportunity along with a fun game. Remember the shortcomings we discussed in our previous section, we must take each shortcomings into account and ideate along those lines to make a simpler game for the younger kids.</p>

<p id="n-a4a62594d832"><strong>Now, let&#x27;s consider how we can create a simpler/easier version of Adopt Me for younger kids:</strong></p>

<p id="n-21c4b85b54b4"><strong>Goal of the new simpler game for Kids: </strong></p>

<ol><li id="n-a71aecb623c3">Easy game for younger audience between the ages 6-8.</li><li id="n-8f2ca2488f20">More learning opportunity of their virtual pets, including dogs, cats, rabbits, and birds.</li><li id="n-a396f44d3f7b">Financial Literacy and empathy.</li><li id="n-fcefa0d3f241">Be one of the largest kids game on Roblox with no fluff and all fun.</li></ol>

<p id="n-8801360de977"><strong>Simplified Gameplay:</strong></p>

<ol><li id="n-0bdff8ae509b">The gameplay should be easier to understand for younger players. </li><li id="n-bcb6db7ce04c">Complex features to remove:<ol><li id="n-2a16d2383181">Trading system.</li><li id="n-e81b9e586618">Grindy nature of the game.</li><li id="n-5cc8cec30f17">Gift Boxes.</li></ol></li><li id="n-0429fe08adc3">Learning objectives and features to add:<ol><li id="n-8388e8cd3810">More focus on taking care of pets and decorating homes. </li><li id="n-202f57b61029">Teaching kids how to take care of virtual pets by feeding them.</li><li id="n-b06a3584b472">Teaching kids about different animal breeds and their characteristics.</li><li id="n-8be3602e54a1">Different care routines for different animals.</li><li id="n-83f478121a4f">Playing with pets and taking them on walks.</li></ol></li></ol>

<p id="n-8cec184dae58"><strong>Learning Objectives:</strong></p>

<ol><li id="n-415b824fbfe3">Educating kids on the following -<ol><li id="n-ebff0267b601">Various breeds of animals and pets they get in the game, and their characteristics. </li><li id="n-037372523d85">Different care routines of each breed through choices.</li><li id="n-a08721da9d5b">What all animals can be domesticated.</li><li id="n-e62ed3b70805">Do’s and Don’ts for various animals using a set of choices.</li></ol></li><li id="n-2c804cd266cf">Educating kids about responsibility, empathy, and financial literacy, encouraging players to save their bucks and make smart purchases to maximize their resources.</li></ol>

<p id="n-9ddf18852db8"><strong>Feedback on each action:</strong> </p>

<ol><li id="n-dc9e5aecd82a">It&#x27;s important to provide feedback to younger players on their actions in the game. We can provide positive reinforcement when players complete tasks like feeding their pets or decorating their homes. </li><li id="n-4328dc7e360e">We will also provide helpful tips and suggestions, and extra bit of knowledge to guide them along the way after they successfully complete a task.</li><li id="n-77e01206f02c">Feedback on each action will focus on taking them closer to the goal of understanding the various breeds and their care routine. </li></ol>

<p id="n-dd215475fad3"><strong>Simple Choices:</strong></p>

<ol><li id="n-a4e7686b3548">We will simplify the choices in the game to make it easier for younger kids to make decisions. For example, when adopting a pet, we can limit the options to a few popular breeds and provide a brief description of each one.</li><li id="n-328b1df37143">Getting a particular breed will remove any uncertainty from buying and hatching eggs. Furthermore, this will also remove the “Gambling” nature from the game.</li><li id="n-62fac29fafbd">Educating kids what the Do’s and Don’ts are for various animals using a set of choices. For example, if a player decided to feed their Dog some chocolates, they will be presented with a set of choices to inform them that dogs should not be fed chocolates.</li></ol>

<p id="n-6ac8a8386ca3"><strong>Design Choices:</strong></p>

<ol><li id="n-32b6fa0c6c2a">The game will have a bright and colorful visual design that appeals to younger children, making the game more attractive and engaging.</li><li id="n-7356b84140c3">The game would have a simplified interface and controls, making it easy for younger children to navigate and play.</li><li id="n-85b5dbca2bb0">Use of a retro, 8-bit, or calming soundtrack and sound effects to make the game soothing to play.</li><li id="n-a4cc72906e14">The game should have parental controls that allow parents to monitor and control their children&#x27;s gameplay and spendings. </li><li id="n-60633cba6154">The game should also have a reporting system in place to allow users to report inappropriate behavior or content.</li></ol>

<p id="n-d0f479fd644a"><strong>Note:</strong> Kids may want to spend in-game currency to buy virtual items, especially if they are passionate about the game and enjoy playing it. However, it is important to ensure that in-game purchases are not necessary to progress in the game or enjoy its core features. In this unnamed game, for example, players can enjoy the game and take care of their pets without spending any money, but they can choose to purchase virtual currency to buy additional items, pets, and accessories to enhance their gameplay experience. It is also important to ensure that in-game purchases are clearly communicated to parents and that parental controls are in place to prevent accidental purchases or excessive spending. I have included a section of my take on financial literacy here: <a class="nm" href="#n-c9a29f326e1e">A Take on Financial Literacy in a Kids Game </a> </p>

<ol><li id="n-b392e1de25bb">Alleged business practice of low revenue shares.</li><li id="n-bc47cd12a7d3">Ridiculous cash-out policies, where the game developers earn their revenue in Robux, which is later cashed out as real-world money.</li><li id="n-9b7f24922c1e">The report from <a href="https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=_gXlauRB1EQ&amp;t=9s" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">People Make Games</a> pointed out that Roblox developers earn 24.5% of their revenue on average, versus 70% on Steam, and 88% on the Epic Games Store.</li></ol>

Having such a big community means that a lot of games are competing for attention on the platform, and discoverability on Roblox is a challenge. This means we will have to allocate more resources to the marketing and sales team. Let us discuss some strategies that can be followed to maximize our chances at a well-received game on the Roblox Platform.</p>

## A Take on Financial Literacy in a Kids Game {#n-c9a29f326e1e}

<p id="n-bc84889dac80">Although we would not want to use Trading in the game, we will use Monetization through in-app purchases to teach kids about financial responsibility in a very easy way, at an young stage. Incorporating elements of money management and savings into the in-app purchases of the game could be a valuable educational feature. Here are some potential ideas for how to implement this:</p>

<p id="n-3eb934b3c3be"><strong>Concept of virtual currency:</strong> At the start of the game, players could be introduced to the concept of virtual currency and how it can be used to buy different items in the game. The game could explain that players earn virtual currency by completing tasks and achievements in the game or by purchasing it with real money.</p>

<p id="n-55f35f60786f"><strong>Set savings goals:</strong> The game could encourage players to set savings goals by displaying the cost of virtual items and encouraging players to save up to buy them. <em>For example, the game could offer a special breed of an animal/pet that costs 1,000 virtual coins, and players could be encouraged to save up to buy it at instead of spending their coins on other items. </em>The game could reward players for saving their virtual currency by offering bonuses or discounts on certain items when purchased with saved coins. <em>For example, the game could offer a 10% discount on a pet bed if purchased with saved coins instead of purchased with real money.</em></p>

<p id="n-f9ce9e2c6de7"><strong>Introduce a virtual savings account: </strong>The game could introduce a virtual savings account where players can deposit their virtual coins and earn interest over time. The game could also introduce the concept of earning interest on savings, and explain how saving money can lead to future benefits. The game could encourage responsible spending by displaying the total amount of virtual currency players have earned and spent. The game could also display a breakdown of how much players have spent on in-app purchases, and encourage them to think carefully before making future purchases.</p>

<p id="n-e882ca61051a">By incorporating these elements into the in-app purchases of the game, young kids can learn about money management and savings in a fun and engaging way while still enjoying the game.</p>

<p id="n-abf7ec62e656">Pet Palooza is a fun and an educational game designed for younger children aged between 6 and 8. The game will be available for download on all the platforms supported by Roblox, i.e., iOS, Android, Windows, XBOX, and Amazon Store.</p>
