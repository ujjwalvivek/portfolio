## Deep Analysis of the game, Adopt Me! {#n-448988f7d205}

<p id="n-df13f8e0f0b0">First, let&#x27;s start with an overview of Adopt Me. Adopt Me is a popular Massively Multiplayer Online Role-Playing Game (MMORPG) developed by the Roblox Corporation. Calling Adopt Me a popular game would be a profound understatement. At the time of writing this document, <strong>Adopt Me has amassed a whopping total of a 32.5B visits with a peak of 1.6m+ concurrent players. </strong>That&#x27;s more than 4x the world population! Now let’s break that down, why a game designed for players aged 7+ gained such popularity.</p>

<p id="n-590216ba2151"><strong>Reasons for the Popularity (Mechanics-Wise):</strong></p>

<ol><li id="n-f0bfeb0a0a30">The game was initially launched with a goal where <strong>players would be able to adopt kids</strong>, but later evolved into a game where <strong>players can also roleplay as adoptive parents to different types of virtual pets</strong>, such as dogs, cats, unicorns, and dragons.</li><li id="n-232a1a8a26c1">Kids love <strong>adopting a pet and taking care of them.</strong></li><li id="n-8a16d9075218">The game also allows them to <strong>design their own homes, and explore various locations</strong> in the game&#x27;s world. </li><li id="n-9a8cb128846b">It also has pets in which you can level up by grinding out exp which gives you money to get rarer ones.</li><li id="n-3a106ce2d084">The players are able to <strong>buy and sell pets, and trade items</strong>. </li><li id="n-e07eadf2eaa5">There are also <strong>small little mini-games</strong> to play around the map.</li></ol>

<p id="n-ae6d4127f7b6"><strong>Reasons for the Popularity (Experience-Wise):</strong></p>

<ol><li id="n-9f7d18673c4c">A very polished game with an <strong>attracting art style </strong>that appeals to children.</li><li id="n-bc5d35df9c7e">The <strong>soundtrack and the sound effects</strong> are very fitting.</li><li id="n-3dfc1bbe49cc"><strong>“Family” feel with tasks</strong> that can help players take care of their children and pets.</li><li id="n-38d298f8621f">Family function in which you can <strong>adopt other players</strong>.</li><li id="n-b1357eac3435">It&#x27;s simply <strong>made to chill</strong>.</li></ol>

<p id="n-b525b17c7760"><strong>Reasons for the Popularity (Community-Wise):</strong></p>

<ol><li id="n-a7f80192e12e"><strong>Frequent updates</strong> with a fresh stream of content.</li><li id="n-65bf919ef62b">Combination of an <strong>avid community</strong> of players and <strong>freeform play</strong>, similar to a semi-open world games.</li><li id="n-cffe88b9290b">The game’s <strong>text-chat function</strong> is monitored and <strong>limited</strong> in language, so there’s little risk that children will be <strong>exposed to harmful content</strong>.</li></ol>

<p id="n-a847becb0f18">The <strong>simplicity </strong>is what attracts kids to play the game for hours on hours. It&#x27;s fun to <strong>level up the pet to make Mega Pets</strong> and have an awesome collection. <strong>This evokes a sense of &quot;satisfaction&quot; in many players, compelling them to return for more</strong><strong>. </strong>All in all, the team found a good formula and stuck with it. <strong>These reasons combined</strong> was the <strong>high-poin</strong><strong>t</strong> for the <strong>game’s popularity</strong>. This game rapidly became a <strong>prime social hub</strong> for kids to hangout with friends, or to unwind after a long studious day. The cherry on the top is, <strong>you can do most of these things without making additional purchases</strong> and most players don’t spend anything on the game – <em>but as you progress, there is a drive to sell items that enable you to access rarer pets.</em></p>

<p id="n-ca98aa4d4c5c">With any good product comes a fair share of shortcomings. <strong>According to my findings and playtime</strong>, there is <strong>not much learning</strong> and the game has become more of a <strong>trading platform</strong> to say the least. There are a couple of <strong>scams </strong>that happen <strong>more often</strong> than we’d think. These scams include <strong>cloning a pet, trading for Robux, and levelling up a pet</strong>. </p>

<p id="n-f4fc88cd0891"><strong>Lets discuss some of the shortcomings of the game. </strong></p>

<p id="n-7d3ed99596ed"><strong>Shortcoming #1: </strong>The game is a <strong>grind fest</strong>, where the player needs to put in a<strong> lot of time and efforts</strong> to get a <strong>legendary</strong> pet, which is considered a <strong>very valuable item</strong> in the game. <strong>Mega Neon Diamond Griffin</strong> requires you to put in a minimum of <strong>1170 consecutive days of playing</strong>.</p>

<p id="n-c1dbdbe1fa95"><strong>Why: </strong>There are <strong>five ranks of pets</strong>: Common, Uncommon, Rare, Ultra-Rare and Legendary. The most valuable legendary pets include <strong>Dragons, Unicorns, Griffins, Ninja Monkeys and Kitsunes</strong>. Getting these takes <strong>effort and a lot of time</strong>. Once acquired, you can train your pets by completing tasks. You spend your <strong>entire time micromanaging</strong> your pet (and/or baby) so you can <strong>earn currency to buy things</strong>. Once you have <strong>four fully grown pets</strong>, they can be traded for even <strong>rarer Neon pets</strong> with glowing parts. You can <strong>repeat this process four times</strong> to trade them in for a <strong>Mega Neon Pet</strong> that glows in rainbow colors. As your pet grows, they can <strong>learn tricks</strong>, which are pretty neat until you realize that they add almost nothing other than a <strong>cosmetic value</strong> to the game. </p>

<p id="n-d4d913ac1dcc"><strong>Shortcoming #2: </strong>A kids game primarily <strong>doesn’t</strong> need a <strong>trading</strong> system because of <strong>complexity and unfairness</strong>.</p>

<p id="n-8a4642e4818e"><strong>Why: </strong>Players can <strong>trade pets</strong> with each other. <strong>Trading </strong>may be <strong>difficult </strong>for younger kids to understand, leading to <strong>frustration, confusion, and potential conflicts</strong> among players. Additionally, trading can create an <strong>unfair environment</strong> in games, especially if some players have <strong>access to better items or pets</strong> than others, leading to <strong>feelings of exclusion or resentment</strong>. There are also safety concerns, as trading may encourage players to <strong>share personal information</strong> or engage in risky behaviors to obtain rare items, putting them at risk of <strong>cyberbullying or online predators</strong>. </p>

<p id="n-92973efc85b8"><strong>Shortcoming #3: </strong><strong>In-App purchases</strong> to have an edge, bringing you closer to <strong>“winning”</strong> the game. This creates a sense of <strong>“Gambling”</strong> touch to the game. This whole thing can be termed as a <strong>“Pay-2-Win”</strong> situation.</p>

<p id="n-e644bb1a69bf"><strong>Why: </strong>Players receive money from <strong>daily award, periodic checks, and completing pet tasks</strong>. However, unless they <strong>play every single day</strong>, its a <strong>slow and long grind</strong> to get more expensive things. This ecosystem of pets <strong>encourages extended play</strong> time as well as the <strong>purchasing of currency</strong>. While the game may be <strong>virtual</strong>, the money is <strong>real</strong>. Eggs, <strong>Gift Boxes</strong>, etc. that have a posted 2-8% chance of getting a rare or a legendary items. This brings in a <strong>“Gambling”</strong> touch to the game. The game also present the player with the <strong>premium items first</strong> discouraging them from <strong>designing their in-game avatar</strong>. If they&#x27;re not paying attention, they will <strong>think </strong>that those are the <strong>only items in the entire catalog</strong>. Furthermore, other concerns are <strong>scams </strong>and <strong>price-hiking</strong>, with items deliberately priced to make players buy <strong>more currency</strong> than they <strong>require</strong>. This is a classic example of <strong>Ladder Strategy</strong>. <em>Ex: A pet might cost 1000 Robux, but you can only buy 800 ($9.99) or 1700 ($19.99) Robux at a time. </em></p>

<ol><li id="n-30c53f7a7c06">According to a report from <a href="https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=_gXlauRB1EQ&amp;t=9s" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">People Make Games</a>, Roblox sees an estimated <strong>200 million users a month</strong> and its core user base is between the <strong>ages of 6 and 15</strong>.</li><li id="n-89568d4b7c9e">Roblox is like a <strong>blank canvas</strong> to start with, giving a <strong>creative freedom</strong>.</li><li id="n-a754166e61d1"><strong>Easier</strong> to create <strong>multiplayer games</strong> without dealing with <strong>complicated network code or server hosting</strong>.</li></ol>

<p id="n-de25c80fcc29">We will <strong>leverage these advantages</strong> of Roblox as a platform to create a new game for <strong>kids between the ages 6 and 8</strong>, giving them <strong>more learning opportunity</strong> along with a <strong>fun game</strong>. Remember the shortcomings we discussed in our previous section, we must take each <strong>shortcomings into account</strong> and <strong>ideate along those lines</strong> to make a <strong>simpler game</strong> for the younger kids.</p>

<ol><li id="n-a71aecb623c3"><strong>Easy game</strong> for younger audience between the <strong>ages 6-8</strong>.</li><li id="n-8f2ca2488f20">More <strong>learning opportunity</strong> of their <strong>virtual pets, including dogs, cats, rabbits, and birds</strong>.</li><li id="n-a396f44d3f7b"><strong>Financial Literacy </strong>and <strong>empathy</strong>.</li><li id="n-fcefa0d3f241">Be one of the <strong>largest kids game</strong> on Roblox with <strong>no fluff and all fun</strong>.</li></ol>

<p id="n-8cec184dae58"><strong>Learning Objectives:</strong></p>

<ol><li id="n-415b824fbfe3">Educating kids on the following -<ol><li id="n-ebff0267b601"><strong>Various breeds of animals</strong> <strong>and pets</strong> they get in the game, and <strong>their characteristics</strong>. </li><li id="n-037372523d85"><strong>Different care routines</strong> of each breed through choices.</li><li id="n-a08721da9d5b">What all animals can be <strong>domesticated</strong>.</li><li id="n-e62ed3b70805"><strong>Do’s and Don’ts</strong> for various animals using a <strong>set of choices</strong>.</li></ol></li><li id="n-2c804cd266cf">Educating kids about <strong>responsibility, empathy, and financial literacy</strong>, encouraging players to <strong>save their bucks</strong> and make <strong>smart purchases</strong> to maximize their resources.</li></ol>

We can provide <strong>positive reinforcement</strong> when players complete tasks like feeding their pets or decorating their homes. </li><li id="n-4328dc7e360e">We will also provide <strong>helpful tips and suggestions, and extra bit of knowledge</strong> to guide them along the way after they <strong>successfully complete a task</strong>.</li><li id="n-77e01206f02c">Feedback on each action will <strong>focus</strong> on taking them <strong>closer to the goal</strong> of <strong>understanding </strong>the <strong>various breeds and their care routine</strong>. </li></ol>

## A Take on Financial Literacy in a Kids Game {#n-c9a29f326e1e}

<p id="n-bc84889dac80">Although we would <strong>not want to use Trading in the game</strong>, we will use <strong>Monetization through in-app purchases</strong> to teach kids about <strong>financial responsibility</strong> in a very easy way, at an young stage. Incorporating elements of <strong>money management and savings</strong> into the in-app purchases of the game could be a <strong>valuable educational feature</strong>. Here are some potential ideas for how to implement this:</p>

<p id="n-3eb934b3c3be"><strong>Concept of virtual currency:</strong> At the start of the game, players could be introduced to the <strong>concept of virtual currency</strong> and how it can be used to buy different items in the game. The game could explain that players <strong>earn </strong>virtual currency by <strong>completing tasks</strong> and <strong>achievements</strong> in the game or by <strong>purchasing it with real money</strong>.</p>

<p id="n-55f35f60786f"><strong>Set savings goals:</strong> The game could encourage players to <strong>set savings goals</strong> by displaying the cost of virtual items and <strong>encouraging players to save up</strong> to buy them. <em>For example, the game could offer a special breed of an animal/pet that costs 1,000 virtual coins, and players could be encouraged to save up to buy it at instead of spending their coins on other items. </em>The game could <strong>reward players</strong> for saving their virtual currency by <strong>offering bonuses or discounts on certain items</strong> when purchased with <strong>saved coins</strong>. <em>For example, the game could offer a 10% discount on a pet bed if purchased with saved coins instead of purchased with real money.</em></p>

<p id="n-f9ce9e2c6de7"><strong>Introduce a virtual savings account: </strong>The game could introduce a <strong>virtual savings account</strong> where players can <strong>deposit </strong>their virtual coins and <strong>earn interest</strong> over time. The game could also introduce the concept of <strong>earning interest on savings</strong>, and explain how saving money can lead to <strong>future benefits</strong>. The game<strong> </strong>could encourage responsible spending by <strong>displaying</strong> the <strong>total amount of virtual currency</strong> players have <strong>earned and spent</strong>. The game could also display a <strong>breakdown </strong>of how much players have <strong>spent on in-app purchases</strong>, and encourage them to <strong>think carefully</strong> before making future purchases.</p>

<p id="n-e882ca61051a">By incorporating these elements into the in-app purchases of the game, <strong>young kids</strong> can <strong>learn </strong>about <strong>money management</strong> and <strong>savings </strong>in a fun and engaging way while <strong>still enjoying the game</strong>.</p>
